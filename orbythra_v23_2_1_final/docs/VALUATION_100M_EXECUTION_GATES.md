# ORBYTHRA — $100M Valuation Execution Gates

This is an execution framework, not a valuation guarantee. A $100M company valuation requires market evidence beyond source code.

## Gate A — Internal product baseline

Status target: repository-controlled.

- Production API and PostgreSQL topology.
- Cryptographic trust and key-rotation architecture.
- GDPR-aligned privacy engineering.
- Enterprise provider registry and policy enforcement.
- Reproducible benchmark scorecards.
- Usage/metering data.
- Security CI and release evidence.
- Procurement/security documentation.

## Gate B — Live production proof

Required evidence:

- Public/private enterprise production endpoint tied to immutable release SHA.
- KMS/secret-manager-backed production keys.
- Centralised logs/alerts and operational dashboard.
- Backup restore exercise with measured RPO/RTO.
- Measured uptime/latency/error-rate over meaningful operating window.
- Deployment rollback test.

## Gate C — Independent validation

Required external evidence:

- Independent product/engineering validation (for example the planned Radianode process).
- Independent penetration test and remediation/retest report.
- Independent benchmark witness or reproducible third-party evaluation.
- No claims are marked complete until the external party issues the evidence.

## Gate D — Enterprise customer proof

Target sequence:

1. 3–5 design partners in high-consequence AI use cases.
2. Predeclared 30-day pilots with customer datasets and acceptance criteria.
3. At least 3 converted paid enterprise contracts.
4. Referenceable case studies where customers permit disclosure.
5. Demonstrated expansion or renewal after first contract period.

Measure: time-to-value, evidence-pass improvement, unsupported-claim reduction, traceability coverage, incident reduction, evaluator hours saved, renewal/expansion.

## Gate E — Recurring revenue engine

A defensible high valuation usually requires recurring commercial evidence. Internal planning target: build toward multi-million-dollar ARR with strong growth, gross margin, low churn and enterprise expansion. Do not present a revenue target as achieved revenue.

Track: ARR/MRR, ACV, gross margin, pipeline coverage, win rate, sales cycle, logo churn, net revenue retention, CAC payback, support cost per account and usage expansion.

## Gate F — Assurance moat

Build a compounding asset that is harder to copy than code:

- Versioned failure corpus and negative controls.
- Cross-provider benchmark history.
- Customer-specific policy templates.
- Evidence/provenance evaluation datasets with rights/provenance documented.
- Remediation patterns and regression library.
- Patent/legal review of potentially protectable inventions, while preserving third-party licenses and prior-art analysis.

## Gate G — Enterprise trust program

- SOC 2 readiness then independent Type I/Type II as commercially appropriate.
- ISO 27001 scope/certification path if buyer demand justifies it.
- Executed DPA and subprocessors/transfer process.
- SSO/SCIM and enterprise identity lifecycle.
- Vulnerability disclosure/management process.
- Formal business continuity and incident exercises.

## Board-level evidence rule

Every claimed milestone must point to one of: release SHA, CI result, signed customer contract, invoice/payment evidence, independent report, measured production telemetry, audit/certification output, or customer-approved case study. Internal tests must never be presented as independent certification or revenue proof.
