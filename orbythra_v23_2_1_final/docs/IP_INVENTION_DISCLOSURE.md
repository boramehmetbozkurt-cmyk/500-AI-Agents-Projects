## Required invention record for each family

For each candidate, preserve: problem statement; earliest conception date/evidence; named contributors; diagrams; prior versions; technical difference over known approaches; experiments; failed alternatives; public disclosures; repositories/commits; third-party dependencies; customer disclosures under NDA; and commercial use cases.

## Clean-IP rules

- Do not claim ownership of third-party/open-source components.
- Preserve all applicable licenses and attribution.
- Separate ORBYTHRA-authored code/know-how from inherited/public code.
- Record contributor assignments before external investment or acquisition diligence.
- Review any prior public disclosure before filing strategy.
- Run prior-art and freedom-to-operate review with qualified patent counsel before asserting patentability.

## Commercial moat beyond patents

Protectable value should also include trade secrets, benchmark/failure corpus, evaluation methodology, customer policy templates, reproducible evidence workflows, integration know-how and operational data. Access to confidential moat material should follow least privilege and NDA controls.
