# ORBYTHRA Data Processing Addendum — Working Template

Status: **legal-review-required template; not an executed agreement.**

## 1. Roles and scope

The commercial order form must identify the customer and ORBYTHRA contracting entity and state, for each processing activity, whether the customer acts as controller and ORBYTHRA as processor or whether another allocation applies. Processing is limited to documented customer instructions and the services described in the order form.

## 2. Processing details

- Subject matter: enterprise AI verification, evidence processing, engineering intelligence, pilot evaluation, auditability and related support.
- Duration: contract term plus documented retention/deletion period.
- Data subjects: customer users, authorised workforce, end users and other persons represented in customer-provided data as applicable.
- Data categories: configured by customer; special-category data is disabled by default unless a documented lawful condition and additional safeguards are established.
- Purpose: solely the contracted service purposes and documented customer instructions.

## 3. Confidentiality and security

Personnel and authorised subprocessors with access to personal data must be subject to confidentiality obligations. Technical baseline includes encryption in transit, application-layer encrypted data at rest, RBAC, workspace isolation, audit logging, rate limiting, secure CI gates, key rotation capability, incident register and retention controls.

## 4. Data-subject requests

ORBYTHRA technical controls support access/export, rectification, restriction, consent-withdrawal state and erasure for data registered in the privacy subsystem. The controller remains responsible for validating identity, legal applicability, exemptions and response content unless the parties agree otherwise.

## 5. Subprocessors

Approved subprocessors must be listed in the subprocessor register with service purpose, processing location, transfer mechanism where applicable and notification/change-management terms. No production subprocessor should be represented as approved until actually contracted and reviewed.

## 6. International transfers

Where personal data is transferred outside an applicable protected jurisdiction, the parties must document a valid transfer mechanism, supplementary measures where required and the relevant transfer impact assessment. EU Standard Contractual Clauses may be used where legally appropriate after counsel review.

## 7. Incident cooperation

ORBYTHRA maintains an incident/breach register and a 72-hour review clock for potential personal-data breaches. This is an operational escalation control and does not replace the controller's legal notification assessment.

## 8. Return and deletion

At termination or documented instruction, personal data must be returned or deleted subject to legal retention duties, backups and agreed deletion windows. Production contracts must define backup deletion timing and evidence of deletion.

## 9. Audit and evidence

Reasonable security/compliance evidence may include architecture documentation, security test results, audit logs, dependency/security scan outputs and independent reports when available. Formal certifications must only be claimed after issuance by the responsible independent body.

## 10. Required commercial completion fields

Before signature, complete: contracting entities; controller/processor allocation; processing instructions; data categories; data-subject categories; retention periods; subprocessors; regions; transfer mechanism; security schedule; incident contacts; DPO/privacy contacts where applicable; liability; governing law; audit terms; deletion evidence requirements.
