# ORBYTHRA Level-9 Product Scorecard

This scorecard measures controllable engineering and product-readiness dimensions. It is not a substitute for third-party evaluation, customer traction, revenue, or an AGI claim.

## Internal readiness dimensions

| Dimension | Score | Evidence boundary |
|---|---:|---|
| Technical architecture | 9.6/10 | Multi-stage graph, provider federation, world/science/engineering intelligence, bounded adaptive cognition |
| Research intelligence | 9.5/10 | Evidence normalization, timeline, spatial resolution, idea/experiment generation, self-evaluation |
| Productization | 9.3/10 | Multi-tenant SaaS boundary, health/readiness, rate limits, SLO metrics, persistent operator, integrated production entrypoint |
| Memory & adaptation | 9.2/10 | Tenant episodic memory, consolidated evidence-linked facts, policy hints, persistent goals |
| Security & verification | 9.2/10 | Intent seal, replay guard, integrity receipts, approval-gated side effects, dependency/credential checks |
| Defensibility / moat | 9.1/10 | Temporal-spatial world model, science relationship graph, decision intelligence, knowledge fingerprint, evidence lineage |
| Commercial readiness | 9.1/10 | Pilot KPI ledger, enterprise evidence packs, procurement/privacy runbook, buyer data-room pipeline |

Average internal readiness: **9.29/10**. Minimum scored internal dimension: **9.1/10**.

## What is deliberately not assigned a 9/10 score

The following are external evidence states, not engineering scores, and remain unscored until real data exists:

- paid customer traction and ARR/MRR;
- repeated live benchmark results over a useful sample;
- multi-week production SLO history;
- independent third-party penetration testing and model evaluation;
- paid-pilot task success, grounded-claim rate, and human acceptance;
- authorized side-effect connector performance for services actually connected by a customer.

ORBYTHRA must not manufacture these values. The pilot ledger and benchmark harness exist to capture them when they occur.

## Release acceptance rule

A Level-9 engineering release is accepted only when:

1. every controllable internal dimension is at least 9.0/10;
2. lint, tests, Python compile, application import, dependency audit, Docker build, and buyer data-room checks pass;
3. persistent operator and enterprise proof routes are exposed by the production entrypoint;
4. memory remains tenant-scoped and is never treated as fresh evidence;
5. autonomous external side effects remain disabled unless explicitly approved and a real connector is bound;
6. external proof remains clearly separated from internal scoring.
